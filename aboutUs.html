About Us

At Tabby and Tea we are a family business and we believe life is better with cats! Our café was created so you can sip a yummy drink while spending time with our lovely friendly cats. Bring family and friends, or just come by yourself and share a moment of joy with the kittens.

We partnered with local adoption centers to give our feline friends a safe, loving environment, and to help them find their forever homes. Each cat who joins our café has their own story, and we’re honored to be part of their journey.

Tabby and Tea is not just about the cats, it’s about the people who walk through our doors. With every cup of chai, matcha, or coffee, we hope to serve not just a drink, but a moment of calm and connection.
